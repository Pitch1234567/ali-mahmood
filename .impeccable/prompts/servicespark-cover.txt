Cinematic concept-project cover image for ServiceSpark, a home-services website case study. A severe modern dark residence at blue hour, warm interior window light, restrained landscaping, and one thin icy cyan illuminated path leading clearly toward the entrance as a visual metaphor for a shorter path to enquiry. Premium architectural photography, near-black and slate palette, realistic materials, no people, no text, no logos, no interface mockup. Wide 16:10 composition.
