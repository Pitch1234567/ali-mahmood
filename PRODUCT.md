# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are small-business owners and founders evaluating whether Ali Mahmood can create a professional website for their business. Secondary users are hiring reviewers who want to assess front-end judgment, implementation quality, and attention to detail.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio helps a visitor quickly understand what Ali builds, how he approaches the work, and how to start a project. Success means the visitor can understand the offer, inspect honest examples, and reach the contact experience without ambiguity.

## Positioning

Ali combines clear business-facing communication with responsive front-end craft. The portfolio demonstrates that approach through its own interface instead of relying on unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is a single-page website with six anchored sections: Home, About, Services, Work, Process, and Contact. Visitors may arrive on desktop or mobile, use keyboard or touch navigation, prefer reduced motion or transparency, and inspect local concept notes before deciding whether to contact Ali.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- The first version is a polished frontend experience. It does not send email or fetch GitHub data.
- The contact form validates on the client and server, delivers through Resend when configured, and reports an honest setup error otherwise.
- ServiceSpark, FocusFlow, and TableReady are concept projects, not client work.
- Missing email, GitHub, LinkedIn, repository, and live-site destinations are shown only as noninteractive pending states.
- The page includes exactly two purposeful dotLottie moments with static fallbacks.
- A hint of 3D means layered, responsive DOM composition rather than a WebGL scene.
- Location and availability are intentionally omitted until Ali confirms them.

## Brand Commitments

- Public name: Ali Mahmood.
- Role: Web developer.
- Voice: clear, professional, direct, and honest.
- Primary action: Start a project.
- Secondary work action: View selected work.
- Visual direction: a single dark Night Glass world with restrained cyan interaction color, careful material depth, generous space, and no generic neon developer-template treatment.
- The hero uses a clearly labeled non-identifying portrait placeholder until Ali supplies a real photograph.

## Evidence on Hand

- `PRD.md` supplies the initial audience, offer, services, project concepts, and copy direction.
- No real portrait, client case studies, project repositories, live project URLs, social URLs, contact address, testimonials, or performance outcomes are currently available. Missing public destinations are omitted until verified values are configured.
- Generated imagery must be labeled as concept material wherever a visitor could mistake it for real project evidence.

## Product Principles

1. Demonstrate craft through the interface itself.
2. Make the offer and next action obvious.
3. Present concept work honestly and avoid fabricated proof.
4. Keep animation purposeful, interruptible, and accessible.
5. Preserve a fast, semantic experience beneath the visual treatment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The implementation must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, reduced transparency, increased contrast, touch input, 200 percent zoom, and layouts down to 320 pixels wide.
